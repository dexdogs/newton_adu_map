# Homecoming // dexdogs

An interactive map that measures the back yard of every home in the selected city and shows where a detached accessory dwelling unit (ADU) could physically fit.

## What are the datasets used?

All public: MassGIS Level 3 assessor parcels; City of Newton GIS — building footprints, zoning, streets, easements, historic districts, floodplain, wetlands, spot elevations, town boundary.

- https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-property-tax-parcels
- https://github.com/NewtonMAGIS/GISData

## Why Newton, MA?

Newton adopted an ADU ordinance on April 22, 2025 that goes beyond state law — detached ADUs up to 1,000 sq ft by right, 1,500 by special permit, no additional parking — plus it publishes unusually complete open GIS.

- https://www.figcitynews.com/2025/04/city-council-revises-rules-for-accessory-dwelling-units/

## What is this map?

It measures the back yard of every Newton home and shows where a detached ADU physically fits — after subtracting the house, setbacks, front yard, and easements — then filters by owner, terrain, and lot.

Computed from the datasets above.

## Why is this map?

Since 2014, Newton has permitted an average of 10 ADUs per year, and just 0.6% of single- and two-family homes have one — the right exists citywide but almost no one acts on it. This makes it concrete per parcel.

- https://bcheights.com/218544/metro/newtons-zoning-and-planning-committee-holds-public-hearing-on-proposed-amendments-to-accessory-dwelling-unit-ordinance/

## Who cares?

Homeowners weighing a rental or in-law unit; small builders sourcing prospects; the city and advocates estimating capacity. Councilors framed the ordinance as helping families, people with disabilities, and older adults stay in their homes.

- https://www.newtonbeacon.org/city-council-passes-adu-ordinance/

## Why does it matter?

From 2025 to 2035, Massachusetts needs at least 222,000 more homes to achieve housing abundance. ADUs add homes with minimal disruption; the binding constraint is knowing where they fit. This map answers that.

- https://www.mass.gov/info-details/a-home-for-everyone-progress-toward-222000
